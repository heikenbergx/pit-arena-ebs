Heik's Battle Arena — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Who this covers

Heik's Battle Arena ("the Extension") is a Twitch Extension that displays a viewer's in-game character and gear for the interactive chat game running on the streamer's channel. This policy explains what information the Extension handles and why.

What we collect

  • Your Twitch username / user ID — only if you choose to share your identity with the Extension (the "Link my Twitch name" button, Twitch's Identity Link feature). This is used solely to find and show your own character.
  • Your in-game data — the character progress you create by playing the game in chat: level, gold, XP, equipped gear, armor sets, and stash items. This is generated by playing; it is not sensitive personal information.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your email address, real name, payment information, IP address, or location.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics (no Google Analytics or similar).
  • We do not read your chat messages or view your video.

How your information is used

The information above is used for one purpose only: to display your own character and gear inside the Extension panel while you watch the stream. If you never share your Twitch identity, the panel simply shows a sample loadout and stores nothing about you.

How it is stored and shared

In-game data is stored on a private helper server operated for this Extension, keyed to your Twitch username, only so your loadout can be shown back to you. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with any third party. Your identity is shared with the Extension only through Twitch's official Identity Link feature, and only after you opt in.

Data retention and deletion

In-game data persists so your character is remembered between sessions. It may be reset when the streamer starts a new season. To have your data removed at any time, contact us using the address below and we will delete your stored character data.

Children's privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by Twitch's Terms of Service in your region), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the Extension after a change means you accept the updated policy.
